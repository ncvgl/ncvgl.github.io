What I'm Looking For Welcome! You arrived here because you sent me an offer about a role. I get quite a few messages about roles in AI, so I figured it would be easier to write down what I'm looking for. I'm currently an AI FDE at Google. I joined at L5 (Senior) three years ago and I'm now operating at L6 (Staff) level. Before Google, I was a technical co-founder and CTO for several years. I raised $10M in total, built a company from 0 to 1 and managed two engineering teams of 5 people. The company is still running. First thing: I'm not looking for another FDE job. Google is the best workplace I have ever seen. The compensation is very good, the people are great, my manager is awesome, the work is interesting, the customers are nice, and the environment is very healthy. If I leave for somewhere else, it is for a step up, for an opportunity that is meaningfully bigger. What does “bigger” mean? Mostly technical difficulty, meaningful scope, and real ownership. I want to work on hard problems and have enough scope to actually own them. That could mean Research Engineer working on things like coding agents, pretraining, RL, inference or agentic systems or Tech Lead taking a new AI product or capability from 0 to 1. I'm completely open to moving away from customer-facing FDE work. I'm equally happy being a deeply technical IC or leading a team if that's what the scope calls for. I'm particularly interested in opportunities at ambitious AI companies and frontier AI labs, especially where engineering and research overlap. Compensation For context, My minimum threshold to consider moving is $450k cash , simply because my total compensation at Google is above that. For a startup, I'd also expect equity, but I think of that as upside for taking startup risk, rather than as a replacement for cash compensation. One practical constraint I'm based in the UK on a Skilled Worker visa, which also covers my family. Any move to a UK employer would therefore need appropriate visa sponsorship. I'm happy taking startup risk and have done it before. But because a job change also affects my family's immigration status, I'm looking for companies where I can see myself building a real, long-term working relationship. I’m not looking for a situation where I join and three months later discover that the team or the role isn’t the right fit. TL;DR I'm not looking for a slightly better version of my current job. I'm looking for a step up: a much bigger technical challenge, with real ownership. If you have something like that, let’s chat! Link to my tech blog -> ncvgl.com
